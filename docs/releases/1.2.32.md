# 1.2.32 — bounded pacing with scheduler headroom

The capped packet scheduler retains eight milliseconds of bounded credit so
ordinary multi-ms runtime wakeups do not starve the selected video rate. The
1.2.31 two-ms candidate failed installed software-codec qualification and was
not promoted. A deterministic four-ms-wakeup regression reproduces its rate
underdelivery and protects this correction.

The bitrate ceiling is unchanged: bounded encoder peak up to1.5×video target,
plus40% repair/header allowance and1Mbps audio/control. A50Mbps video selection
permits71–106Mbps paced QUIC, with at most106,000bytes saved credit at the maximum
budget. Idle time cannot accumulate a larger burst. Uncapped controllers keep
the upstream pacing policy. Codec/FEC/queue and authentication settings remain
unchanged; compatible Clients need no update.

This bounds sender bursts. It does not establish the cause of a particular
network freeze or add adaptive congestion backoff. See the
[pacing contract](../development/bitrate-pacing.md).
